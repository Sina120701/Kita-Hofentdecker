Discover great things with little hands.

More than just vegetables grow here.

Experience nature – learn with all your senses

At our planned Hofentdecker daycare centre, nature will be an integral part of everyday learning. The spacious outdoor grounds offer children many opportunities to explore their surroundings independently and with all their senses.

From seed to harvest

In our planned raised beds and orchard, children will sow, water, care for plants and finally harvest the produce themselves. Along the way, they can take responsibility, develop patience and experience natural cycles firsthand.

Discover, explore and understand

By watching insects, collecting natural materials and experimenting with soil and water, we want to nurture children’s natural curiosity. Their own experiences, independent exploration and individual interests are at the heart of our approach. Those who discover things for themselves learn for life.

Explore indoors, harvest outdoors

The renovated farm offers space for a nursery group, two groups for older preschool children, an art studio, a workshop and a spacious activity room. In the studio, children can express themselves creatively; in the workshop, they can build with wood and develop their own ideas. The activity room offers space to play and move even on rainy days. Our fruit trees let children see how blossoms become fruit they can pick themselves. In our small farm shop, we plan to sell eggs and seasonal fruit and vegetables from our own harvest.

Meet animals – learn responsibility

Animals are intended to play an important role in daily educational life. Gentle, respectful contact with them can strengthen children’s empathy, social development and emotional well-being.

Our animal companions

Three goats and ten chickens will give children the opportunity to get to know animals up close. With guidance from educators trained in animal-assisted activities, children can help feed and care for them, take responsibility and develop respect for living beings. Chewie, our educational companion dog, is planned to visit once a week so that children can meet him voluntarily and build trust at their own pace. Chewie is 11 years old.

Our farm is ready

We are preparing to open Hofentdecker daycare at an already renovated farm in Elsdorf-Westermühlen. We hope to use the funding to equip the rooms for everyday educational activities. We already have support: Beetliebe is providing seeds free of charge; the organic tree nursery Pflanzenlust is offering fruit trees at a reduced price; and the Materialhof carpentry workshop is supporting our children’s workshop with leftover wood and joint projects.

Our guiding principles

Every child belongs at Hofentdecker and can help shape everyday life with their questions and ideas. When children sow, harvest or meet our animals at their own pace, they experience that their actions make a difference and that they can take responsibility.

Our vision

We want children to grow up confident in their own abilities and to treat other people, animals and nature with care.

Our mission

We organise daily life so children can help make decisions and get involved. Whether sowing seeds, caring for animals or working in the workshop, they discover what they can achieve on their own and together.

Our five values

Community · Responsibility · Participation · Independence · Mindfulness
